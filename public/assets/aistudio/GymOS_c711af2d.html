Module 05 · Programs and home gym

Twelve weeks.
One clear path.

A transformation program with measurable targets, and a home gym planner that fits your budget.

W1-4

Foundation

Learn the lifts at RPE 6 to 7. Three sessions a week. Calories at maintenance. Protein 1.8 g per kg. Take baseline photos, weight, waist and a 5 rep test.

W5-8

Build

Add a fourth day. Raise sets to 12 to 16 per muscle. Add 2.5 kg or one rep each week. Deficit or surplus of 300 to 500 kcal to match the goal.

W9-12

Peak

Heaviest sets at RPE 8 to 9. Deload in week 12. Re-test your 5 rep max, repeat photos and measurements, then compare against week 1.

Hardware
